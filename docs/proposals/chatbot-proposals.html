★ NEW · LIVE CHAT TAKEOVER

A real person can step into any chat

The AI answers visitors on its own. When the site owner or their team sees a conversation that needs a human touch, they can take it over from the dashboard and reply themselves.

  1. A visitor is chatting and the AI is answering.
  2. In the dashboard, a team member opens the live chat and clicks Take over.
  3. The visitor sees that a team member has joined. Everything the team member writes appears in the visitor's chat.
  4. While a person is in charge, the AI stays silent and doesn't answer automatically.
  5. When done, the team member clicks Hand back to AI, and the chatbot answers again.

★ NEW · SMART SALES ALERTS

The AI tells you when a visitor is ready to buy

The owner describes the situations that matter, in plain words. The AI watches every conversation. When a situation happens, it sends an instant message to the team by email, Telegram or Slack, with a short summary and a Take over link. The team joins the chat live and turns the visitor into a customer.

Example situations the owner can set

  • Visitor asks about a bulk order, a quote or a business price
  • Visitor asks for a discount or coupon
  • Visitor asks to speak to a person
  • Visitor is unhappy or complaining
  • The AI couldn't answer a question twice in a row

Extra situations for WooCommerce shops

  • Visitor has a large cart but hasn't checked out
  • Visitor keeps asking about the same product (price, size, delivery)
  • Visitor asks about a product that is out of stock
  • Visitor seems stuck at checkout or asks about payment
  • A returning customer is back with a question

Works together with Live chat takeover: the alert brings the team in at the right moment.

The two proposals

Both include all the features above. The only difference is who provides the AI and what that needs.

PROPOSAL 1

Customer uses their own AI account

Everything runs on the customer's website. They connect their own AI account (such as OpenAI or Google) and buy a license from us for the full version.

What we add for this option

  • Choice of AI providerWorks with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google and others. The customer pastes their own key.
  • Usage limitsCaps so the customer's AI account is never overused.
  • License and automatic updatesA license key unlocks the full version and keeps it updated.
  • Free and full versionsA free version on WordPress.org, with an upgrade to the full version.

Good and less good

Good

  • Data goes only to the customer's own AI account
  • Customer chooses the AI
  • Nothing for us to keep running

Less good

  • Setting up an AI account is hard for non-technical owners
  • Customer manages a separate AI bill
  • Many competitors already work this way
PROPOSAL 2

We provide the AI as a subscription

The website's content stays on the customer's website. The answering AI runs through our own service, so the customer never needs an AI account. They connect their site and subscribe.

Both together? Proposal 2 could be the default ("works out of the box"), with an option for agencies to use their own AI account as in Proposal 1.
