Lead Form in Chat: Plan

Built 18 Nov 2026 (LF0–LF6, commits on master; deviations in the md, section 13). Source: docs/lead-form-plan.md · Requirements: docs/lead-form-requirements.html

How the lead form gets built: an owner-built form in the chat, triggers that run in the visitor's browser, a new leads table, a team-member button in Live chat, email alerts, and a Leads tab with CSV export.

LeadCard.tsx

  • Inputs from config, labels, required, min/max, autocomplete hints.
  • Same checks in the browser as on the server; server field errors under each field.
  • Focus moves into the card only when the visitor or agent opened it, never on automatic triggers (typing is never interrupted). Esc closes.
  • Lead entries skipped in completedTurns() / contextQuery(): the AI never sees them.
  • "New conversation" clears the card but not mnaisp-lead-v1.

11Review questions all answered

  1. Off vs ManualSplit: switch "Show the lead form" + "When to show it" with Manual.
  2. Leads tab admins onlyYes. Editors send the form and see answers in the live thread.
  3. Pre-chat requiredYes, as an option (no ✕, input locked); default not required.
  4. EmailDefault Always, same addresses as the other alerts.
  5. Spam limits3 per minute, 10 per day per visitor.
  6. Header iconShown from the start.
  7. Consent checkboxOff by default, with an EU / GDPR hint.

12Built: deviations from the plan

  • Validation errors use the existing 400 with fields, not 422.
  • CSV export returns { filename, csv }; the admin app saves the file. Formula-looking cells get a leading '.
  • "Email me new leads" sits with the other email alerts (Live chat tab), same three choices, default Always.
  • Pre-chat card shows after the welcome message.
  • Deleting a field has no confirm dialog; the card says old leads keep their answers.
  • RateLimiter::allow_daily() added; leads.event_id indexed; lead_closed written once in a row.
  • GET /live/chats returns leadForm; the Conversations viewer also lists leads sent before the first question.
