Lead Form in Chat: Requirements

Requirements only, 18 Nov 2026. How it will be built is planned later. Source: docs/lead-form-requirements.md · Related: commercial-requirements D2, D4

At the right moment the chatbot shows a small form inside the chat asking for name, email and phone. The visitor can fill it in or close it. A closed form never comes back by itself, but the visitor can always open it again.

What was asked When to show The form Close & reopen Site owner Privacy Quality Decisions Not in scope

1What was asked

  1. A form inside the chat with name, email, phone and a line asking the visitor to fill it in.
  2. The visitor can close it without filling it in.
  3. Once closed, it never shows up again by itself.
  4. A visitor who closed it and kept chatting can open it again later.
  5. When to show the form is not decided yet. This is the main open question (sections 2 and 9).
  6. The form is not hardcoded: the owner builds it from 4 field types (Text, Number, Email, Textarea). Name, email, phone is only the default.

2When to show the form decided 18 Nov

Not at a fixed point. "End of chat" can't be detected: visitors rarely say goodbye, they just leave. Asking on the first message interrupts them before they got any value. So the form shows when it is earned or when the moment fits, and the site owner picks the rule.

3The form

  • Inline in the transcript as a bot message card, not a pop-up. The chat input stays usable: the visitor can ignore it and keep typing.
  • Short text above the fields, owner editable ("Want us to follow up? Leave your details.").
  • Fields: built by the owner (below). Default: name, email, phone.
  • Consent checkbox with a privacy-policy link (GDPR); the owner can turn it off.
  • Checked in the browser and again on the server: required fields filled, Email fields valid, lengths within limits.
  • After submit the card becomes a thank-you line (owner editable). Widget colours from the Style tab.

Form builder decided 18 Nov

The owner builds the form in admin: add, remove, reorder and edit fields, with a live preview of the card. Four field types:

Rules

  • Each field has a stable ID; renaming a label keeps old answers attached.
  • Each lead stores the label as it was, so deleting a field never breaks old leads.
  • At least one field. Max 10 fields.
  • Phone = a Text field, not Number (+, spaces, leading zeros; no format check).
  • Consent checkbox = built-in switch, not a field type.

1. "Fill in" line
On close the card shrinks to one line in the transcript. That line reopens the form right where the visitor left it.
2. Header icon
A contact icon in the chat header always opens the form (when the lead form is on), even after closing or submitting.
3. Soft second chance
When the bot later can't answer, a small "Leave your details" link under that answer. A link only, never the full form pushed again.
"Never again" lasts
Closed / submitted is remembered in localStorage (not sessionStorage), so it holds across tabs, pages and later visits on the same browser. After submit, the header icon still lets the visitor update details (saved as a new lead on the same conversation).

5What the site owner gets

Stored lead

New leads table, linked to the conversation (what the visitor asked) and the page where the form was sent. Saved even when recording is off, then without the conversation link and transcript.

Leads tab

List: date, page, conversation link, plus the answers (first Email and first Text field as columns, the rest in the detail view). Search, CSV export (one column per field label), delete one or many.

Email per new lead

Through the existing alert system (AlertService / EmailChannel): details, short transcript, link to the conversation.

In Conversations

A "Left contact details" marker with the details. Later (P1): analytics for forms shown / submitted / closed.

6Privacy

  • Lead data is personal data: WordPress personal-data exporter and eraser (commercial-requirements I1).
  • Privacy-policy text via wp_add_privacy_policy_content, like the conversation log and analytics.
  • No IP address stored (same rule as the conversation log).
  • Leads are kept until the admin deletes them. No retention setting, no automatic purge. The WordPress personal-data eraser still deletes a visitor's leads on request.

7Quality

  • Form code loads with the chat, not in the tiny loader: page speed unchanged.
  • Works with page caching: state in the browser, saved through the REST API.
  • Spam: honeypot (the widget has one) + per-visitor rate limit (existing RateLimiter).
  • Keyboard and screen reader: labels, focus moves into the form, Esc / ✕ closes.
  • Floating and shortcode chat, desktop and mobile. WWC branch too, when merged on request.

8Decisions all answered 18 Nov

  1. Default trigger answeredAfter 2 answers + can't answer + offline handoff. No buying-word trigger for now.
  2. Required fields answeredOwner decides per field in the form builder.
  3. Message field answeredOwner adds a Textarea field if wanted (not in the default form).
  4. Recording off answeredLeads always saved, recording on or off. Recording off = no conversation link, no transcript in the email.
  5. Live chat answeredNo automatic form during live chat; the team member's "Ask for contact details" button opens it in the visitor's chat. Offline-handoff trigger stays.
  6. Retention answeredKept until the admin deletes them. No retention setting.
  7. Free vs Pro answeredNo split. One plugin, everything included.
  8. Logged-in visitors answeredNo pre-fill. Empty fields for everyone.

9Not in scope (now)

  • CRM / newsletter integrations (Zapier, Mailchimp, FluentCRM, webhook): D4, later.
  • More field types: checkbox, select / radio, phone with format check, date, file upload.
  • Conditional fields (show a field only when another has a value).
  • Multi-step conversational forms (bot asks one field at a time).
  • Lead scoring.
  • Buying-word trigger (price, quote, demo, contact, call): dropped 18 Nov, maybe later.
