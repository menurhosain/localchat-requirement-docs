## What it does
Thin WP connector to Jotform's hosted AI Agent. Create, train, publish chatbot without code.
FAQ/product answers, guides visitors, walks users through forms conversationally.
Persona, tone, colors, fonts, avatar. Page visibility rules. Conversations page in WP admin
(view + reply).

## Features
Agent Builder, auto-training (crawls site, auto-syncs new/updated posts/pages/FAQs),
industry templates, multilingual auto-response, form assistance, escalation rules,
custom URL, custom avatar, Enterprise login. Free vs paid split not on page.
