## What it does
Chatbot trained on own site content (pages, posts, PDFs, URLs). Answers visitors, captures
leads, hands off to humans.

## Features
Free: multiple bots, no-code builder (purpose, tone, behavior), floating or inline widget,
shared KB across bots w/ auto-sync, appearance customizing, tone presets, greeting/fallback,
50+ languages, conversation viewer w/ search, basic insights, moderation/security settings,
human handover.

Handover: AI detects need for human; channels Email, WhatsApp, Telegram, IG, Messenger;
sends chat summary to team; auto-trigger after N unanswered questions.

Pro: advanced analytics, lead capture (FAQ says Pro-only; changelog says Beta), integrations.
