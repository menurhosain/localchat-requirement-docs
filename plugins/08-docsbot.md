## What it does
Thin connector for DocsBot.ai SaaS (known standalone AI-support product). Settings > DocsBot:
greeting, design, page/visitor rules, feedback, human handoff, web search, booking, custom buttons.

## Knowledge base
"Trained on your content" — details live on SaaS side, not on WP page. Account + API key required.

## Relevance
- Shows SaaS players treat WP as just an embed channel; the WP plugin itself is new and tiny.
- Weakness: no native WP content sync described; data on third-party servers.
